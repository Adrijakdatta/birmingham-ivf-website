FAQs

Common questions about iui treatment

What is IUI?

A simple treatment that places prepared sperm directly into the uterus at the most fertile time. It is chosen by single women, same-sex couples, some couples with unexplained infertility, and men with erectile dysfunction.

What are the benefits of IUI?

IUI usually needs few or no fertility drugs, can use donor sperm from regulated banks, costs much less than other treatments, and is quick, with minimal discomfort and usually no sedation.

Is IUI guaranteed to work?

No treatment is guaranteed. IUI offers a realistic chance, and many people succeed over one or more cycles.

Who is IUI suitable for?

Single women, same-sex couples and heterosexual couples with mild or unexplained fertility problems. After a certain age, IVF is usually more suitable.

Is IUI painful?

Most people feel only mild cramping, similar to a period or a smear test.
