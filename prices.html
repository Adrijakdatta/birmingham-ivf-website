FAQs

Questions about cost and payment

When do we need to make the payment?

The full cost of treatment is paid at or before your treatment consultation with the nurses.

Are medication costs included in the IVF packages?

No. Medication costs vary widely with your treatment protocol, so they aren’t included. Medication for an IVF cycle usually costs £800 – £1,000 on average, and you’ll get a full breakdown of every medicine.

Are there extra charges I need to know about?

At your initial consultation we give you a detailed estimate for the treatment we recommend. If your protocol needs to change to keep your chances as high as possible, extra medication or blood tests may be needed. Any change is discussed with you before anything is actioned.

Can I reduce the cost?

We don’t add services or add-on treatments that aren’t scientifically proven to benefit you. You get a clear breakdown of all costs, and we show which items are optional.
