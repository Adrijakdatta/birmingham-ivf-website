FAQs

Questions about fertility testing

How long is the appointment?

Female and couples assessments take 60 minutes. Virtual consultations take 30 minutes.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You don’t need a referral for an assessment or consultation.

What should I bring?

Any previous investigations, such as blood tests or semen analyses, for you or your partner.

Can my partner join a virtual consultation?

Yes, and we strongly encourage it.

Can a female clinician do my internal scan?

Dr Datta performs the detailed scans himself and prefers to carry out follow-up scans too. A female scanner will be available soon and can be arranged at a later date.
