Medical Director & Founder

Dr Adrija Kumar Datta

30 years as a gynaecologist and more than 18 years in assisted reproduction.

Dr Datta has particular expertise in treating couples in challenging circumstances, and is one of the world’s leading advocates of mild approaches to IVF, which aim to reduce both the burden and the cost of treatment.

Over the last 10 years he has focused on women of advancing age and those with premature ovarian insufficiency. Where possible, he offers women the chance to conceive with their own eggs before considering donor eggs.

Fertility tests and treatments in Birmingham

Every plan is built around your results, your age and your preferences. We only recommend what is scientifically shown to help.

Fertility tests

A one-stop assessment in a single visit: detailed 3D ultrasound, ovarian reserve and hormone tests, and semen analysis where needed.

IVF treatment

Mild and Natural cycle IVF, with or without ICSI, tailored to how your body responds. Fewer injections, similar live birth rates.

IUI treatment

The least invasive fertility treatment, well suited to single women, same-sex couples and mild or unexplained infertility.

IVF using donor eggs

When your own eggs aren’t an option, donor egg IVF with fully screened UK, international or known donors.
