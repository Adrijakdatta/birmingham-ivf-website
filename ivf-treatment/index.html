Step by step

Your IVF journey

  1. Initial consultation60 min

    A full consultation with advanced ultrasound. Further hormone tests (AMH, FSH, LH, testosterone) and routine infection screening are arranged only when needed.

  2. Pre-treatment follow-up15–30 min

    A session with Dr Datta to finalise your treatment plan.

  3. Treatment protocol

    Mild stimulation using a short protocol, or a natural or modified-natural cycle, with luteal phase support.

  4. Nurse consultation

    Usually virtual. Covers consent forms, your detailed plan, self-injection training and arranging medication.

  5. Monitoring scans3–4 scans

    Every other day from around day 5 or 6 of your cycle, to track follicle growth.

  6. Egg collectionDay case

    In theatre under sedation or anaesthesia. You’ll need someone to take you home after a few hours’ rest.

  7. Embryo transfer

    Usually five days after egg collection. Any extra good-quality embryos are frozen.

  8. Pregnancy test

    A urine test at home or a blood test on a set date after transfer.

  9. Pregnancy scanFrom 6 weeks

    Confirms the pregnancy is viable and in the right place.

  10. Counselling & follow-up

    Available at any stage, including after treatment.

Additional procedures

ICSI and PGT-A

ICSI

A single sperm is injected directly into each egg. Mainly used for male factor infertility, though not only for that.

PGT-A

Genetic testing of embryos, offered only in specific circumstances and never as routine. See the HFEA’s guidance on treatment add-ons for more.

FAQs

Common questions about ivf treatment

What is gentle IVF?

Gentle IVF uses lower doses of stimulation drugs, generally 150 IU of FSH a day or less, to encourage fewer but healthier follicles. Trials show live birth rates similar to conventional IVF, with a gentler and more affordable experience.

Is gentle IVF as effective as conventional IVF?

Randomised trials show no difference in live birth rates per fresh cycle or in cumulative live birth rates.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can refer yourself. If you have a referral letter or earlier test results, bring them along, but they aren’t needed to book.

I’m over 40. Is IVF with my own eggs suitable?

Treatment with your own eggs may be offered up to age 46 where it is reasonable. Live birth with your own eggs beyond 46 is almost unheard of, so we don’t consider it ethical to offer treatment after that age.

How will IVF affect my daily life?

Most patients keep working. Stimulation lasts 5–9 days rather than 10–14, with fewer injections and milder symptoms, and gentler approaches have a 45% lower dropout rate.
