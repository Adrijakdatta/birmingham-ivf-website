Step by step

Your ivf using donor eggs journey

  1. Initial consultation60 min

    A full consultation with advanced ultrasound. Further assessment of the womb lining may be arranged, with routine screening for hepatitis B and C, HIV and rubella.

  2. Pre-treatment follow-up30 min

    With Dr Datta to finalise your plan and choose how your womb is prepared: a natural cycle, a medicated cycle with oestrogen, and luteal phase support.

  3. Donor nurse consultation

    Usually virtual. Covers donor matching, consent forms, your written treatment plan and medication.

  4. Monitoring scans1–2 scans

    From around day 7–9 of your cycle, to check your womb lining.

  5. Embryo transfer

    A fresh transfer is usually planned five days after the donor’s egg collection. Extra good-quality embryos are frozen.

  6. Pregnancy test

    A urine test at home or a blood test on a set date after transfer.

  7. Pregnancy scanFrom 6 weeks

    Confirms the pregnancy is viable and in the right place.

  8. Counselling

    Sessions before and after treatment, and a follow-up appointment if the result is negative.

FAQs

Common questions about ivf using donor eggs

Who can use donor eggs?

Anyone who can’t use their own eggs because of age, medical treatment, low egg quality or genetic concerns.

How are donors selected?

Every donor has medical and genetic screening. You can use a known donor, a UK donor or an international donor.

What affects success rates?

Mainly the quality of the donor’s eggs (donors are usually young), the health of your womb, and the fertilisation and transfer process.
