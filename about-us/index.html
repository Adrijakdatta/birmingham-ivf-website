Medical Director & Founder

Dr Adrija Kumar Datta

  • MDDoctor of Medicine, Obstetrics & Gynaecology
  • FRCOGFellow of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists
  • EditorEditorial Board, Fertility & Sterility Reviews
  • ISMAARResearch Coordinator, Board of Directors
  • LecturerHonorary Senior Lecturer, University of Greater Manchester

Dr Datta has 30 years of experience as a gynaecologist and more than 18 years in assisted reproduction. He has particular experience with couples in challenging circumstances, and is a leading international advocate of mild IVF, which aims to reduce the burden and cost of treatment.

For the last 10 years he has focused on women over 40 and women with premature ovarian insufficiency. Where possible, he offers the chance to conceive with your own eggs before donor eggs are considered, and he puts real weight on emotional support throughout treatment.

He believes pregnancy rate alone shouldn’t be the measure of IVF success. A holistic approach can make treatment safer and more affordable. A 2021 meta-analysis in Human Reproduction Update found no difference in pregnancy outcomes between mild and conventional IVF, and ISMAAR now recommends that mild stimulation could replace conventional stimulation to make IVF safer and more accessible worldwide.
